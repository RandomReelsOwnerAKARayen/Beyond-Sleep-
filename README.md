# Beyond Sleep

*A journey through memories, dreams and the space between life and waking.*

Beyond Sleep is a story-driven 2D precision platformer about a boy trapped in a mysterious world between consciousness and reality. Explore surreal environments, uncover forgotten memories and discover the truth behind a world that is slowly falling apart.

## 📖 Story

You play as **Eli**, a 15-year-old boy who falls into a coma after an accident.

When he wakes up, he finds himself somewhere unfamiliar. A strange world known as **The Between**.

The Between is a mysterious dimension formed from the memories of people trapped in unconsciousness. Its landscapes are constantly changing, combining fragments of memories, dreams and forgotten places.

During his journey, Eli meets **Mira**, a mysterious girl who claims to have been trapped in The Between for years.

She tells Eli about a place known as **The Door**, supposedly the only way out.

Together, they travel through strange environments, uncovering memories and secrets that slowly reveal the truth about Eli's accident, Mira and the world itself.

But something is wrong.

The world is beginning to erase itself.

And Eli might have been here before.

## 🎮 Features

- **Story-driven gameplay** – Discover a mysterious story through exploration and environmental storytelling.
- **2D precision platforming** – Navigate challenging environments and obstacles.
- **Atmospheric environments** – Explore surreal landscapes built from fragments of memories.
- **Mystery and exploration** – Discover hidden details and uncover secrets about The Between.
- **Memories and flashbacks** – Piece together fragments of Eli's forgotten past.
- **Mysterious characters** – Uncover the truth behind Mira and her connection to Eli.
- **Multiple possible endings** – Your journey can lead to different outcomes.

## 🌍 The World

The Between is made up of fragments of memories belonging to unconscious people.

Some places feel familiar, while others seem impossible.

Possible environments include:

- **The Bedroom** – A familiar place that slowly becomes distorted.
- **The School** – A maze-like environment filled with fragmented memories.
- **The Floating Beach** – An unusual landscape surrounded by endless ocean.
- **The Forgotten Places** – Abandoned environments hiding pieces of the past.

## 👥 Characters

### Eli
A 15-year-old boy who wakes up inside The Between after falling into a coma. He must uncover what happened to him and find a way out.

### Mira
A mysterious girl Eli encounters in The Between. She claims to have been trapped there for years and guides him towards The Door.
